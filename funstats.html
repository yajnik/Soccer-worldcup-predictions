As the tournament progressed, the group collectively dialled back their predicted scorelines — group stage picks averaged 3.05 goals per match; knockout predictions averaged 2.67. The margin calls got even tighter in the semis (avg 1.03 goals difference). The irony: actual knockout margins turned out bigger than group stage ones. France beat Morocco 2–0, England beat Argentina 2–1, and England beat France 4–6 — far more goals than anyone predicted. The group became more cautious exactly when football became more explosive.

A consistent pattern: the group always backed Argentina to win, but always predicted fewer goals than they scored.
